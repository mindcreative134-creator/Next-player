Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 1, 2026

Creative Mind Studio ("we", "us", or "our") operates the mpvExtended Pro mobile application (the "Service", package name: com.nextplayer.pro). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personally identifiable information from your device. The Service is designed to function primarily as a local media player for playing video and audio files.

Types of Data Processed

  • Local Media Files: The Service requires access to your device's media storage to discover, list, and play video, audio, and subtitle files. All media processing occurs locally on your device. Your local media files and private data are never collected, stored, or transmitted to external servers.
  • Usage Data: We may collect non-personally identifiable information on how the Service is accessed and used. This Usage Data may include information such as your device's Internet Protocol address (IP address), device type, operating system version, and app usage statistics.

2. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device for advertisement and analytics purposes.

  • Google AdMob: Our Service uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data such as your device's Advertising ID to deliver relevant ads and prevent fraud. For more information on how Google manages advertising data, please visit: How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

3. Permissions

mpvExtended Pro requests the following Android permissions strictly to deliver its core functionality as a media player:

  • Media & Storage Access (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Required to discover, browse, and play local video, audio, and subtitle files stored on your device.
  • Internet Access & Network State: Required to load advertisements (via AdMob) and handle network streaming (e.g., SMB, FTP, WebDAV, HLS) initiated by the user.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): Used by Google AdMob to provide appropriate ad content.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK): Allows continuous audio and video playback when the app runs in background mode.

4. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
