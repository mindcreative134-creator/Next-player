NextPlayer is a powerful, lightweight, and modern HD & 4K video player designed to give you seamless media playback on your Android device. With support for all popular video and audio formats, advanced hardware acceleration, subtitle customization, floating Picture-in-Picture mode, and network streaming, NextPlayer is your complete offline and online media companion.

Key Features:

🎬 Universal Format Compatibility
• Plays all major video formats: MKV, MP4, AVI, MOV, FLV, WMV, 3GP, TS, WebM, RMVB, and MPG.
• Supports high-resolution audio codecs including MP3, AAC, FLAC, WAV, OGG, and Opus.
• Multi-track audio and subtitle selector for movies with multiple languages.

⚡ Smooth HD & 4K Playback
• Enjoy ultra-smooth playback for Full HD (1080p), 2K, 4K Ultra HD, and HDR videos.
• Hardware-accelerated decoding engine optimizes battery efficiency and delivers fluid frame rates.
• Customizable aspect ratios: Fit to screen, Crop, 16:9, 18:9, 21:9, and Original.

💬 Advanced Subtitle Customization
• Full support for external and embedded subtitle formats: SRT, ASS, SSA, VTT, SUB, IDX, and PGS.
• Real-time subtitle synchronization and delay adjustments.
• Full styling control: customize font size, colors, outline, shadow, and screen positioning.
• Dual Subtitle Mode: display primary and secondary subtitles simultaneously.

🎵 Floating Window (PiP) & Background Playback
• Picture-in-Picture (PiP) mode lets you watch your favorite videos in a floating window while browsing or chatting.
• Background audio playback allows you to listen to podcasts, concerts, and videos even when the screen is locked.

🚀 Intuitive Smart Gestures
• Easy swipe controls to adjust volume, screen brightness, and playback seek position.
• Smooth pinch-to-zoom to inspect fine details in any video.
• Screen lock toggle to prevent accidental touches during playback.

🔒 Secure Private Video Vault
• Protect personal videos in a password-protected, encrypted secure folder.
• Unlock your hidden media using PIN or Biometric / Fingerprint authentication.

🌐 Local Network & Online Streaming
• Stream media directly from your home network servers via SMB, FTP, and WebDAV protocols.
• Direct URL playback support for HTTP, HTTPS, HLS (m3u8), RTSP, and RTMP streams.

📂 Clean Media Library & Folder Browser
• Automatically indexes all video and audio files from internal storage and SD cards.
• Browse videos in customizable Folder, Tree, or Library views with A-Z sorting and quick search.
• Track your playback progress and resume videos right where you left off.

Permissions Notice:
• Media / Storage Access: Required to locate and play video files stored on your device and SD card.
• Foreground Media Service: Required for background audio playback and lock-screen notification controls.
• Network Access: Required for network share streaming (SMB/FTP/WebDAV) and direct online stream playback.
